ABOUT US

Professional security. Dependable protection.

JSB Security Services is an Ontario security agency focused on providing professional, dependable and client-focused security solutions.

Our goal is to provide businesses and property owners with a security partner they can rely on.

We believe effective security begins with understanding the client's environment. That means understanding the property, identifying security requirements, establishing clear procedures and maintaining consistent communication.

At JSB, we aim to combine a professional security presence with dependable service and clear communication.

OUR MISSION

To provide professional security services that help our clients protect their people, property and operations while maintaining high standards of professionalism and service.

OUR VISION

To become a trusted security services provider throughout Ontario by building long-term relationships with clients and consistently delivering dependable security solutions.

Professional Security. Built on Reliability.

JSB Security Services provides professional security solutions for businesses, properties, construction sites, events and organizations throughout Guelph, Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge and surrounding Ontario communities.

01 / WHO WE ARE

Security Services You Can Depend On.

JSB Security Services is focused on providing dependable, professional and client-focused private security services.

We understand that every property, business and organization has different security requirements. Our approach begins with understanding the environment, identifying the client's needs and providing appropriate security coverage.

From visible security personnel and access control to mobile patrols, event security and property protection, our focus is on professionalism, communication, reliability and a credible security presence.
