01 / LOCAL COVERAGE

Serving Guelph & Surrounding Ontario Communities

JSB Security Services is based around the Guelph and Kitchener-Waterloo-Cambridge region and serves surrounding communities based on client requirements and availability.

Our local service approach allows us to support a range of security requirements for commercial properties, construction sites, industrial facilities, retail environments, events and managed properties throughout the region.

Security requirements can vary from one location to another. We work with clients to understand the property, operating schedule and type of coverage required before determining suitable service options.
